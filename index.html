A quick trail guide.

Two pedals. A whole lot of possibilities.

Drive as far as you can without landing on your roof or running out of fuel. Collect coins, upgrade your jeep, and come back for one more hill.

01 / Find your rhythm

D / → A / ←

Right or D accelerates. Left or A brakes, then reverses. On a phone, hold the onscreen pedals. Both pedals together give a stronger brake.

02 / Stick the landing

In the air, gas lifts the nose and brake lowers it. Ease off before a crest and line up your wheels with the landing. Long jumps and full flips earn bonus coins.

03 / Keep the tank happy

Red fuel cans refill your tank. Gold coins are worth 5 each. Every 100 meters earns a distance bonus. An empty tank lets you coast briefly to a final refill.

04 / Make it a habit

P or Escape pauses, R restarts, and M toggles sound. Your best distance and coins save between runs. Banked coins buy engine, tire, suspension, and fuel upgrades.
